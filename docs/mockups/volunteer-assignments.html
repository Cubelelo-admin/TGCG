Assigned desks and picker items: interactive mockup

The admin assigns each desk volunteer a desk and each picker their items in Configuration → Volunteers. Volunteers can't change these on their own screens; pickers keep Start / Pause. Change an assignment in the table and the phone on the right shows what that volunteer would see. In the real app, the phone updates within about 10 seconds. The phone opens mid-task, on the new picking screen: tap the "Camera sees" buttons next to it to scan, and watch each step replace the last.

New or changedExample emails and placeholder runners only: nothing here touches the database.

Admin · Configuration → Volunteers

Decisions this mockup makes (tell me if any are wrong)

  1. A picker with no items can't Start. They see "No items assigned yet" until the admin assigns some. (The alternative: an unassigned picker picks everything.)
  2. Changes reach the phone by themselves. A blue note says what changed. A task already on the picker's screen carries on; the new items apply from the next offer.
  3. The 90 s overflow stays. A task for an item the picker wasn't given says "Not one of your items · waiting over 90 s", so they know why.
  4. A desk volunteer with no desk gets no scan box, only "No desk assigned yet".
  5. Two desk logins can share one desk (e.g. two laptops at D1); both see D1's orders.
  6. Admins previewing /v keep the desk switcher and the item ticks, because admins aren't assigned and need them for testing.
  7. Accept splits the task screen. Top, the larger part (over half the screen): the live scanner, with what to scan now written across it, and a box right under it to type a code when the camera can't read. Below: the current step, then a progress bar pinned to the bottom ("Step 2 of 3", items picked, order → pigeonhole). The Start / Pause card is hidden while picking (Pause is greyed out during a task anyway). "Camera off" folds the scanner away and leaves the box; if the phone blocks the camera, it starts that way. After the pigeonhole scan it goes back to the normal screen.
  8. One instruction at a time. The bottom half shows only the current step in large type ("Take 1 × size M from bin TS-M, then scan the bin label"), with Bin is empty and Give task back under it. When a scan finishes the step, the next one replaces it. Steps: T-shirts and packs, one per bin; BIBs, open the bin, then one per BIB; last, the pigeonhole. A scan for a later step still counts; the screen then skips that step.
  9. Scanning picks; there is no Picked button. T-shirts and packs: take them, then scan the bin label, and that bin's items are picked. BIBs: scan the bin label, then each BIB scan picks that BIB.
  10. The last step is the pigeonhole. When everything is picked, the step reads "Drop everything in pigeonhole S-04" in large type; scanning (or typing) that pigeonhole's label finishes the task, and a wrong one is refused.
  11. Alerts say what happened, then what to do now. A bold title and one line under it, with an icon. Red for a wrong scan (bin, BIB, pigeonhole), amber for a warning (no items, not your item, bin empty, taken off your desk), blue for information, green for done. While picking, scan results show over the bottom of the camera, where the picker is looking, not further down the screen. Amber is kept for warnings: ordinary "to do" labels are grey.
